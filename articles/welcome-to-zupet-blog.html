Welcome to the Zupet Blog

August 31, 2026 · By Zupet Team

Welcome! We've started this blog to share everything we know about keeping a healthy, vibrant aquarium — from choosing the right food to building a planted tank your fish will love. Think of it as a friendly companion to the Zupet range.

What you'll find here

We'll publish guides and stories across four main topics:

  • Feeding guides — how much, how often, and which food suits which species.
  • Species spotlights — from Betta splendens to Neocaridina shrimp.
  • Tank setup — step-by-step aquarium builds and water-keeping tips.
  • Behind the scenes — how Zupet food is made.

A quick tip to get started

Healthy fish start with a varied diet. Alternate a daily staple with a color-enhancing or protein-rich food two or three times a week.
