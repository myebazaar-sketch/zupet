Zupet Premium Flakes

Everyday nutrition for a thriving community tank.

Read more Read less

Zupet Premium Flakes are a complete daily staple formulated for tropical community aquariums. Every flake delivers a balanced blend of high-quality marine proteins, essential omega fatty acids, and stabilized vitamin C to support strong growth, vivid color, and long-term vitality. Because the recipe is gently processed at low temperatures, nutrients are preserved and water remains cleaner for longer, reducing the risk of excess cloudiness and ammonia buildup between water changes.

Key benefits

  • Balanced protein-to-fiber ratio supports steady, healthy growth
  • Omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids promote skin and fin health
  • Added vitamin C and D3 strengthen the immune response
  • Slow-sinking flakes reduce surface waste and overfeeding

Key ingredients

Fish meal, krill meal, wheat flour, spirulina, fish oil, vitamin and mineral premix.

Suitable for

Most tropical community fish, including tetras, rasboras, barbs, danios, gouramis and livebearers. Not intended for strict carnivores or bottom-dwelling scavengers.

Usage instructions

  • Feed a small pinch 2–3 times daily, only what fish consume in 2–3 minutes
  • Remove any uneaten food after 5 minutes
  • Store in a cool, dry place and reseal after every use
  • Use within 3 months of opening for maximum freshness

Zupet Color Boost Pellets

Natural color enhancement from the inside out.

Read more Read less

Zupet Color Boost Pellets are a slow-sinking, color-enhancing food formulated with naturally occurring carotenoids that intensify reds, oranges and yellows from the inside out. Instead of relying on artificial dyes, the vibrant color comes from a concentrated blend of spirulina, astaxanthin and Antarctic krill — pigments that are both safe for fish and gentle on water quality. The medium 1.2 mm pellet is sized for mid-water and top-dwelling community fish, encouraging natural foraging behavior.

Key benefits

  • Naturally enhances red, orange and yellow pigmentation within 2–4 weeks
  • High-quality marine proteins support muscle tone and energy
  • Slow-sinking formula reduces surface film and waste
  • Contains added vitamin C to support immune health

Key ingredients

Krill meal, spirulina, fish meal, astaxanthin, wheat flour, fish oil, vitamin and mineral premix.

Suitable for

Livebearers, cichlids, barbs, gouramis, rainbowfish and other color-focused community fish.

Usage instructions

  • Feed once or twice daily, an amount fish consume within 2–3 minutes
  • Alternate with a staple diet for balanced nutrition
  • Remove uneaten food after 5 minutes
  • Store sealed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight

Zupet Spirulina Wafers

A complete veggie wafer for algae-eaters and herbivores.

Read more Read less

Zupet Spirulina Wafers are a complete, fast-sinking plant-based food designed for herbivorous and algae-eating species. Each wafer is packed with over 30% spirulina, a nutrient-dense blue-green algae rich in protein, iron and essential amino acids, making it ideal for supporting the natural grazing behavior of plecos, catfish and shrimp. The dense wafer sinks immediately and holds its shape, allowing bottom dwellers to feed calmly without competition from surface feeders.

Key benefits

  • High spirulina content supports healthy digestion and natural color
  • Sinking wafer format suits bottom and mid-water grazers
  • Contains prebiotic fiber to support gut health
  • Low phosphorus formulation helps maintain stable water parameters

Key ingredients

Spirulina, wheat flour, soybean meal, fish meal, calcium, vitamin premix.

Suitable for

Plecos, otocinclus, Corydoras catfish, snails and dwarf shrimp; also enjoyed by livebearers and goldfish.

Usage instructions

  • Offer one wafer per 3–4 fish or per shrimp colony once daily
  • Break into quarters for smaller tanks or juveniles
  • Remove any wafer not consumed within 24 hours
  • Alternate with a protein-rich food for omnivorous species

Zupet Nano Granules

Micro-sized nutrition for small fish and fry.

Read more Read less

Zupet Nano Granules deliver complete, micro-sized nutrition for small fish, fry and nano-aquarium residents. Every granule is milled to a consistent 0.5 mm size so even the smallest mouths can feed easily, while a high protein content supports rapid juvenile growth and bright coloration. The granules soften quickly in water, making them easy to eat and gentle on developing digestive systems, while the low-dust formulation keeps the surface clear and the water clean.

Key benefits

  • Ultra-fine 0.5 mm granules sized for nano fish and fry
  • High-protein recipe supports steady growth and development
  • Rapidly softens for easy digestion by young fish
  • Low-dust formula reduces surface film and cloudiness

Key ingredients

Fish meal, krill meal, wheat flour, spirulina, fish oil, vitamin and mineral premix.

Suitable for

Nano tetras, rasboras, killifish, livebearer fry, Corydoras fry and newly hatched ornamental shrimp.

Usage instructions

  • Feed small pinches 2–3 times daily, only what fish eat in 2–3 minutes
  • For fry, crush granules between fingers for an even finer crumb
  • Start with a minimal amount and increase gradually
  • Store sealed in a cool, dry place

Zupet Shrimp Delight Sticks

Protein-rich sticks crafted for shrimp and crayfish.

Read more Read less

Zupet Shrimp Delight Sticks are a protein-rich, calcium-fortified food crafted specifically for ornamental shrimp, crayfish and snails. The dense sinking sticks release slowly in water, letting shrimp find and graze at their own pace while minimizing clouding and nutrient leaching. A balanced blend of marine proteins, algae and bioavailable calcium supports strong exoskeleton development, successful molting and healthy breeding in Neocaridina, Caridina and other popular shrimp species.

Key benefits

  • High protein content supports growth, molting and breeding
  • Added calcium strengthens exoskeleton development
  • Slow-release sinking stick reduces waste and water fouling
  • Encourages natural foraging and scavenging behavior

Key ingredients

Fish meal, soybean meal, spirulina, krill meal, calcium carbonate, vitamin premix.

Suitable for

Dwarf shrimp (Neocaridina and Caridina species), Amano shrimp, crayfish, snails and small bottom-dwelling fish.

Usage instructions

  • Offer half a stick per 10–15 shrimp every other day
  • Observe consumption and adjust portions to avoid leftovers
  • Remove uneaten pieces after 24 hours

Zupet Betta Bites

Bite-sized floating pellets made for bettas.

Read more Read less

Zupet Betta Bites are bite-sized floating pellets formulated for the unique needs of Betta splendens and other labyrinth fish. Because bettas feed at the surface and have small stomachs, every pellet is sized to be eaten in a single bite and engineered to float without breaking apart or clouding the water. The high-protein recipe mirrors a betta's natural insect-based diet, supporting vivid fin color, strong swimming and good digestive health while minimizing the bloating risk associated with low-quality foods.

Key benefits

  • High-protein, insect-derived formula matches the natural betta diet
  • Single-bite floating pellet prevents overfeeding and waste
  • Supports vibrant color, fin health and energetic behavior
  • Low-dust recipe keeps the surface film clear

Key ingredients

Fish meal, shrimp meal, bloodworm meal, wheat flour, fish oil, vitamin and mineral premix.

Suitable for

Betta splendens (Plakat, Halfmoon, Crowntail), gouramis and other surface-feeding labyrinth fish.

Usage instructions

  • Feed 2–3 pellets twice daily; soak pellets for 5 minutes first if desired
  • Skip one feeding day per week to aid digestion
  • Remove any uneaten pellets after 5 minutes

  • Store sealed in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight

Our story

Born from a simple question

Zupet began in a small home aquarium workshop with a single frustration: most fish food was packed with fillers, artificial colors and vague promises. We wanted food we could trust for our own fish — so we set out to make it.

Today, Zupet combines aquatic nutrition science with a hands-on love of the hobby. Every recipe is built around real ingredients, reviewed by aquatic veterinarians, and tested in live tanks before it ever reaches your shelf. The result is a range that supports vibrant color, steady growth and clean water — food that works as hard as you care for your aquarium.

From community tanks to specialized breeding setups, our goal is unchanged: premium nutrition that helps every fish thrive, and helps every aquarist feel confident about what they feed.
